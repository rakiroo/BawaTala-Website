1. Purpose of the Platform

Bawat Tala is an academic capstone project (Capstone 1 and Capstone 2) developed to centralize, enhance, and support student wellness at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela. The system offers digital reflective journaling, mood tracking, wellness tools (breathing and grounding exercises), a curated reading library, an AI reflective companion (Muni), and consultation appointment scheduling with authorized PLV Guidance Counselors and Peer Counselors.

2. Important Medical Disclaimer and AI Companion Limits

A. NOT A MEDICAL OR CLINICAL SERVICE: Bawat Tala, its features, and Muni (AI Companion) are designed for self-reflection, emotional tracking, and student wellness support. The platform does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, psychotherapy, or psychiatric advice. Usage of Bawat Tala should never replace professional consultation with a qualified mental health healthcare provider.

B. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE COMPANION (MUNI): Muni is an artificial intelligence (AI) system programmed to provide reflective prompts, summaries, and gentle supportive dialogue. Muni is not a licensed therapist, counselor, or psychometrician. AI responses may occasionally contain errors, inaccuracies, or unexpected phrasing. Users are advised to exercise personal judgment and not rely on Muni for critical personal, medical, or crisis decisions.

3. Emergency Crisis Protocols

BAWAT TALA IS NOT A REAL-TIME CRISIS RESPONSE SERVICE OR EMERGENCY HOTLINE. The mobile app and website are not continuously monitored for live emergencies.

If you are experiencing severe mental health distress, thoughts of self-harm, suicide, or immediate danger to yourself or others, do not wait for an app response or scheduled appointment. Immediately contact official emergency helplines:

  • National Emergency Hotline: Dial 911
  • NCMH Crisis Hotline (National Center for Mental Health): 1553 (Luzon toll-free), 0917-899-8727, or 0966-351-4518
  • Hopeline Philippines: 0917-558-4673 or 2919 (Toll-free for Globe/TM)
  • In-Person Crisis Support: Visit the PLV Guidance and Counseling Office or go to the nearest emergency room.

Access to Bawat Tala is restricted exclusively to currently enrolled students of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV) and authorized institutional staff.

  • ID Verification: Registration requires uploading a clear front image of your valid PLV Student ID card, which is processed via Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to confirm student identity.
  • Truthful Information: You agree to provide accurate, complete, and truthful information during account registration and profile setup. Providing falsified credentials, submitting fake IDs, or impersonating another student is strictly prohibited.

5. Account Security and Student Responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential. Any activity conducted under your registered account is your responsibility.

  • Do not share your password or grant account access to third parties.
  • Notify the Bawat Tala development team or PLV guidance office immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
  • We advise keeping your mobile device secured with pin/biometric locks to prevent local unauthorized access to your journal entries.

6. Safeguarding and Confidentiality Exceptions

While student privacy and journal entries are strictly respected, you acknowledge and agree that student safety is paramount:

  • High-Risk Safety Triggers: When automated risk indicators detect signals of urgent emotional distress (HIGH risk level indicating potential harm to self or others), system alerts are generated for authorized PLV Guidance Counselors.
  • Counselor Outreach: Authorized guidance personnel reserve the right to review high-risk entry summaries and student contact details to conduct proactive wellness outreach and offer counseling support.
  • Safeguarding Logs: High-risk entries deleted by students are hidden from personal view but safely preserved in administrative logs for duty-of-care compliance.
