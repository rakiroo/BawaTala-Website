Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

To keep the community safe, registration requires your student ID. Simply tap 'Register' in the app and upload a clear photo of your PLV ID (front). The app will securely scan your details. Then, just enter your email, set a password, and verify it with the OTP sent to your inbox.

How do I log into the mobile app?

After downloading the APK, open Bawat Tala and enter the student credentials you registered with to access your personal space.

Is Bawat Tala free to use?

Yes, the app is built exclusively for students and is entirely free to download and use.

Are my journal entries private?

Absolutely. Your entries are stored safely and confidentially. Muni reads them only to provide you with soft reflections.

Is scheduling counseling and consultation free?

Yes, scheduling appointments with PLV Guidance Counselors and Peer Counselors through Bawat Tala is completely free for all PLV students.
