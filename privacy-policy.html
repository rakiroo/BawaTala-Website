Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Release: Beta Evaluation & Testing Phase

Welcome to Bawat Tala, a dedicated student wellness app and mental health reflection platform developed specifically for students of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV). This Privacy Policy outlines how the Bawat Tala platform collects, processes, stores, protects, and discloses personal information in strict adherence to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175), and applicable academic guidelines of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela.

This policy applies to all students, peer counselors, school guidance counselors, and administrative users during the Capstone 1 and Capstone 2 development, beta testing, and evaluation period.

1. Scope and Academic Context

Bawat Tala is developed as an academic capstone research project. Its main purpose is to evaluate digital wellness tools, reflective journaling, AI companion interactions (Muni), mood tracking, and guidance consultation scheduling for PLV students. System operations during this period are conducted under controlled academic supervision for testing, feature validation, and user acceptance evaluation.

2. Data We Collect

Bawat Tala collects only data that is necessary for identity verification, system functionality, student wellness support, and academic evaluation. The categories of collected data include:

A. Student Identity and Verification Data

  • PLV Student ID Image: Uploaded front image of your official Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV) Student ID, processed via Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology solely to verify student eligibility during account registration.
  • Institutional & Account Data: PLV Student ID number, full name, email address, password hash, and One-Time Password (OTP) verification tokens.

B. Student Profile and Academic Information

  • Academic Profile: Enrolled academic program (e.g., BS Information Technology, BS Psychology, BS Accountancy, BSEd, etc.), year level, and gender.
  • Demographic & Contact Data: Valenzuela City barangay, mobile contact number, and designated emergency contact details (Name and Phone Number).

C. Journaling, Emotion, and Wellness Data

  • Journal Entries & Metadata: Text entries, entry creation dates/times, custom journal cover settings, selected emotion tags, concern tags, and user responses to support prompts.
  • Muni AI Companion Data: Text messages, voice recordings/transcripts, AI-generated reflections, entry summaries, and emotion analysis generated by Muni.
  • Mood Check-in History: Daily emotional selections, monthly emotion totals, mood calendar check-ins, and Tala star reward accumulations.
  • Safety & Risk Indicators: System-generated risk flags (NONE, LOW - Emotional Distress, HIGH - Urgent Distress), emotion distress scores, and support prompt interactions.

D. Consultation and Guidance Appointment Records

  • Appointment Requests: Selected PLV Guidance Counselor or Peer Counselor, requested appointment date and time slot, preferred mode, consultation concerns, appointment status, and counselor session logs.

E. System Interaction and Activity Data

  • App & Gamification Activity: Unlocked Muni wardrobe items, game scores/achievements (Fruit Catcher, Gentle Pairs, Image Puzzle, Unscramble Word), ambient sound preferences, and wellness library reading progress.
  • Technical Logs: Encrypted local device caches (AsyncStorage), authentication logs, rate-limiting lockout records, and system usage audit trails.

F. Beta Evaluation and Research Data

  • Responses from feature-based surveys, ISO-standard usability questionnaires, and testing evaluation feedback gathered from students and IT experts during alpha/beta testing.

3. How We Use Your Data

All data collected within Bawat Tala during the beta evaluation phase is used strictly for academic, research, wellness support, and system optimization purposes:

  • Authenticating user credentials and enforcing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across Mobile and Admin Web portals.
  • Providing personal wellness features, including emotion calendars, journal history, ambient resets, and Muni AI companion reflections.
  • Facilitating guidance appointment scheduling and peer counseling support between PLV students and authorized guidance staff.
  • Automating safety risk assessment to detect patterns of distress and prompt timely institutional guidance support.
  • Evaluating app usability, performance metrics, and user acceptance for the Capstone 2 Final Defense.

Commercial Prohibition: Bawat Tala does not monetize, sell, lease, or distribute student data to commercial vendors or third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Data Sharing and Confidentiality Exceptions

Your private journal entries and mood logs are treated with strict confidentiality. Access to data is restricted as follows:

  • Bawat Tala Proponents & Evaluators: System developers and faculty advisers have access to anonymized and aggregated testing metrics for research evaluation.
  • Authorized PLV Guidance Counselors: Guidance staff can view appointment schedules, referral logs, and overall wellness analytics to deliver counseling services.
  • Safeguarding Exception (Urgent Safety Risk): When automated sentiment analysis or safety risk indicators identify a HIGH (Urgent) distress level indicating potential harm to self or others, authorized PLV Guidance Counselors (Head Counselor / School Counselors) may review entry summaries, distress flags, and student contact details to initiate proactive wellness outreach and safety protocols.

5. Storage, Security, and Offline Safeguards

Bawat Tala implements technical, physical, and organizational security measures aligned with the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (RA 10175):

  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between the mobile app, admin portal, backend APIs, and database is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Sensitive database records are stored using secure encryption protocols.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) & Access Controls: Database policies restrict record access based on strict user roles and verified authentication session tokens.
  • Local Offline Storage: Journal drafts and mood check-in states are stored in encrypted local device storage (AsyncStorage) to allow offline writing, syncing securely once internet connectivity is restored.
  • High-Risk Retention Policy: If a student deletes a journal entry tagged with a HIGH risk indicator, the entry will be hidden from the student's personal view but retained in administrative safeguarding logs for counselor review and duty-of-care obligations.

6. Retention and Deletion Policy

Data collected during Capstone 1 and Capstone 2 shall be retained for the duration of the development, testing, and institutional evaluation period. Upon completion of academic requirements and final defense presentation:

  • Personal identification data and OCR images may be purged, deleted, or permanently anonymized in accordance with PLV research guidelines.
  • Aggregated and de-identified statistical datasets may be archived solely for academic research documentation.

7. Voluntary Participation and User Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), students participating in the Bawat Tala beta release possess the following rights:

  • Right to be Informed: To understand how personal and emotional data is processed by the app and Muni AI.
  • Right to Access & Correct: To review and update personal profile details, contact numbers, and emergency contacts within the app settings.
  • Right to Withdraw: Participation in beta testing and usability surveys is completely voluntary. Students may discontinue app usage at any time without penalty or academic prejudice.

8. Limitations of System

Bawat Tala is a capstone project undergoing active testing and iterative refinement. Temporary glitches, service interruptions, or AI response inaccuracies may occur during testing. System outputs and AI companion reflections are subject to review by qualified human counselors.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or privacy concerns regarding Bawat Tala, please email us directly at bawattala@gmail.com, visit our Contact Us page, or reach out to the Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela Guidance and Counseling Office.
