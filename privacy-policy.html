Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to Bawat Tala. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app and website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as when you create an account, complete journal entries, log your moods, or communicate with our support team. Your journal entries and Muni interactions are kept strictly confidential.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Bawat Tala app.
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., Muni summaries).
  • Connect you with campus counseling or support services, if you choose to do so.

3. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information and sensitive data. Your journals and moods are yours, and we treat them with the utmost privacy.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact page.
