Campus & Guidance Counseling

Bawat Tala is created for the students of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV) in collaboration with guidance counseling services.

  • Institutional Partner: Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV)
  • Office: Guidance and Counseling Office, PLV Main Campus
  • In-App Services: Enrolled PLV students can also schedule consultation appointments directly through the Bawat Tala mobile app's Wellness and Consultation section.

Emergency Crisis Resources

Please note: Bawat Tala and email support are not monitored 24/7 for acute emergency crisis situations. If you or someone you know is experiencing severe emotional distress, thoughts of self-harm, or an immediate emergency, please contact official helplines right away:

  • National Emergency Line: Dial 911
  • NCMH Crisis Hotline (National Center for Mental Health): 1553 (Luzon toll-free), 0917-899-8727, or 0966-351-4518
  • Hopeline Philippines: 0917-558-4673 or 2919 (Toll-free for Globe/TM)
